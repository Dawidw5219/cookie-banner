About this demonstration

This site demonstrates App4You Cookie Banner with a real YouTube film. This demo does not install analytics or advertising SDKs. YouTube remains disconnected until you choose to allow it. Consent choices are stored in your browser’s localStorage and a first-party consent cookie for up to six months, unless you reset or withdraw them.

After you allow YouTube, your browser connects to Google / YouTube to load the embedded player, and the provider may process your IP address, browser information and playback activity. The player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); this does not mean that loading it sends no data. Retention depends on Google’s policies and your account settings. See Google’s privacy policy and data retention policy. You can withdraw consent using the persistent cookie preferences control.

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which handles network requests according to GitHub’s privacy statement. This demo is not a privacy policy template or a guarantee of compliance for another site.
