About this demonstration

This site demonstrates App4You Cookie Banner with local simulated content. It does not load real video providers, analytics or advertising SDKs. Consent choices are stored in your browser’s localStorage and a first-party consent cookie for up to six months, unless you reset or withdraw them.

The optional local scanner shows accessible cookie names, storage keys and origins of resources loaded by the current page. It does not collect values or upload a report. “Add test data” creates cb_demo_cookie and cb_demo_storage, both containing the constant word example. They do not identify you. The cookie lasts for the browser session and sessionStorage lasts for the tab session. Use “Remove test data” to delete them sooner.

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which handles network requests according to GitHub’s privacy statement. This demo is not a privacy policy template or a guarantee of compliance for another site.
